<div class="thesis-box" markdown="0">
  <p class="kicker">Ph.D. thesis statement</p>
  <p>Precise orbit determination for future small-body missions needs progress on three components together: <strong>dynamical models</strong>, <strong>measurement models</strong>, and <strong>estimation frameworks</strong>. My dissertation addresses each one with (1) a gravity formulation that stays valid near the surface, (2) a map-free terrain-relative optical navigation measurement, and (3) a computationally tractable Bayesian framework that characterizes the full posterior. Together these support safer operations and more science return.</p>
