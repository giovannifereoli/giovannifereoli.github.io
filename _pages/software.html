Open-source software developed as part of Giovanni's research on orbit determination, gravity modeling, and small-body dynamics. All code is available on GitHub.

PythonRustC++

Scarabaeus

An open-source interplanetary orbit determination and navigation framework from the ORCCA Lab, used operationally for the Emirates Mission to the Asteroid Belt. Giovanni has contributed more than 1,000 commits covering dynamics, measurement models, estimation, and verification and validation.

MCMC-STT-OD

Bayesian orbit determination with affine-invariant ensemble MCMC, sped up by high-order state transition tensors.

Interior-Gravity-Field-CH

A cylindrical harmonic (Fourier–Bessel) gravity expansion for modeling small bodies near the surface.

RustPython

GUBAS-RS

A Rust port of the GUBAS binary-asteroid simulator for full two-body problem dynamics, with exact autodiff Jacobians, STM, and parameter sensitivities for OD.

Meta-RL for Proximity Operations

Meta-reinforcement learning (LSTM + PPO) for spacecraft guidance and control in cislunar space. Developed for his M.S. thesis.
