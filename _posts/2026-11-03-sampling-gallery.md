Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) is central to Giovanni's work on [Bayesian orbit determination](/research/mcmc), where full posterior distributions replace the Gaussian approximations of classical estimators. Building intuition for how a sampler moves through a distribution is one of the best ways to understand why some samplers mix well and others struggle.

**[The Sampling Gallery](https://chris-nemeth.github.io/sampling-gallery/sampler.html?algorithm=RandomWalkMH)**, created by **Chris Nemeth**, is an interactive collection of MCMC algorithms. The linked page shows random-walk Metropolis–Hastings, the classic starting point, and the gallery makes it easy to watch a sampler explore a target distribution step by step.
