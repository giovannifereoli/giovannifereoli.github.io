Clear writing is as important to research as clear analysis. Papers, theses, proposals, and technical reports are how results reach other people, and a well-written document is easier to review, cite, and build on.

**[Duncan's Elements of Style](https://duncaneddy.com/writing/duncans-elements-of-style/)**, written by **Duncan Eddy**, is a practical guide to writing technical and scientific prose. It is a useful reference while drafting and revising papers and theses, and a good companion to classic style manuals.
