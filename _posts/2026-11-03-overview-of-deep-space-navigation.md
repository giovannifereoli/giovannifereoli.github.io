Deep space navigation is the foundation of Giovanni's research and of his work on the Emirates Mission to the Asteroid Belt. **An Overview of Deep Space Navigation**, a lecture by **Dr. Shyam Bhaskaran**, Supervisor of the Outer Planet Navigation Group in the Mission Design and Navigation Section at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, is an excellent introduction to the field.

The lecture covers:

- **Background:** why deep space navigation differs from navigation in Earth orbit
- **Mission Design:** planning the route
- **Measurements:** Deep Space Network tracking, Doppler and range data, Delta-DOR, clocks, and optical navigation
- **Orbit Determination:** the navigation process, evaluation of solutions, pre-fit and post-fit residuals, and B-plane mapping
- **Maneuver Design:** closing the loop from orbit determination to trajectory correction maneuvers
- **Examples:** Mars 2020 and Deep Impact
