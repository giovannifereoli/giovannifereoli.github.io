Research

Improving each part of small-body orbit determination

My Ph.D. work, Orbit Determination Methods for Small-Body Gravity Characterization, advised by Dr. Jay W. McMahon, contributes to the three parts of orbit determination: the dynamics model, the measurement model, and the estimator.

01 · Dynamics

Cylindrical harmonic gravity

A Fourier–Bessel interior gravity model that stays valid down to the surface. On Eros it fits the potential with errors below 0.005%, and it supports inference of interior density and local mass changes.

Read more
02 · Measurements

Map-free visual odometry

Epipolar constraints are processed directly in the OD filter, so no landmark catalog is needed and the body's pole can be estimated. Validated on real Dawn imagery of Vesta.

Read more
03 · Estimation

MCMC with state transition tensors

An ensemble MCMC sampler that uses high-order flow expansions instead of repeated propagation, making full non-Gaussian posteriors tractable and exposing biased linearized solutions.

Read more
